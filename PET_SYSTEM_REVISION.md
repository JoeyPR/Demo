# Pet engine port revision (SetPlannerGT source-assisted)

This revision replaces the old approximate follower (`src/pet-motion.mjs`, `demo/engine/pet-motion.mjs`) with a direct port of the SetPlannerGT pet engine, extracted from the deobfuscated `app.js` of the live planner bundle ([application](https://setplannergt.com/app.d5e8585e60.js)). The port is source-assisted and verified against the live canvas where noted; it is **not** official-client verified. No invented item data was added: every constant, table and formula below traces to the source bundle or the planner catalog.

Read this after `ARCHIVE_MOUNT_PET_REVISION.md` and `STATE_ROUTING_REVISION.md`. The old `pet-motion.mjs` statements about a "bounded follower" are superseded.

## What changed

- **`src/pet-system.mjs` (new, mirrored at `demo/engine/pet-system.mjs`)**: a full port of the source `PlayerMotionController` pet step (`petOwn`/`petStates`/`petPoint`/`petMove`/`petCellNow`/`petString` and the kind table). One `PetEngine` instance per pet item, state persisting across frames on the engine.
  - **Trail pets (kinds 0..38)**: chase a 20-tick trail of behind-points (`TRAIL=20`, `TICK=1/60`, `TRAIL_SPEED=350`, `BEHIND=16`, `HALVE=20`, snap 60/250/350 per kind family). The pet only starts moving once the trail flushes (~21 ticks) — this is the source's observable follow lag, not a bug.
  - **Flyers (kinds 1/3/9/23/25/27)**, **hovers (11/31/32)**, **kites (4/30/34)**, **perch (5/35)**, **hop (28/29)**, **ball (18)**, **owlbeard (10)**, **phoenix (17)**, **agent (37)**, **walkRunJump and flyWalk (38)** all dispatch as in the source.
  - **Machine pets (kind 200)**: wolf (11704/11706), dragon (11562), Crimson Thief Mask (13116) and Captain Bark (13116-family config, item 15336 uses flyWalk instead) with the source state machines (`PET_MACHINES`), including the wolf's punch leap toward `punchAim`.
  - **Pet cells**: `petCellNow` samples `stand/move/rise/fall` rows on the pet's **own clock** (`t68`), selected by the pet's last-tick displacement and ground state. Cells are absolute (`cell[0]*sw`, `cell[1]*sh`), verified against the live canvas (wolf resting cell `(0,13)` reads `(0,704)` on `tf_page1.png`).
  - **Leash string**: kinds 4/30 draw a line primitive from the pet to the arm-anchored hand point (`petString`), rendered by `render.mjs` as a canvas line (`lineCap: round`, default rgb `[180,180,180]`). The piece is flagged `petLine` and cleaned up every step.
  - **Machine extras**: authored-space copies of the main piece flagged `petExtra`, rotated around the main pin, cleaned up every step. Emitted only by the non-dragon machines at their hover-at-target state (the wolf's spinning piece); the source's dragon branch selects cells directly and emits no extra (verified against the live bundle `app.8f20650106.js`).
  - **Punch**: `PunchDelay` publishes 1 on a punch event and decays on the pet clock; the wolf leap targets the punch aim. The sprite flip follows the source `SQ`: `(petPunching ? hostFacingLeft : petFaceLeft) XOR hostMirror` — while the pet shows punch cells (none ship in the current catalog) it faces the player. The previous aim-side guess (`punchAimLeft`) was removed as unsupported.
- **`src/catalog-adapter.mjs`**: imports `pet-system.mjs` (old `pet-motion.mjs` deleted from both trees). Machine pets without `gameIcon` pet records get a generated 64×64 piece from their `parts` rect (`sx: rect[0], sy: rect[1]-32`, anchor 0.5/0.5 at the part's authored `at`) — verified against the live canvas. `advancePet` now runs **before** `advanceXmlRenderer` so scripted states (Captain Bark) can read the pet variables.
- **`src/xml-renderer.mjs`**: `variable()` resolves flat `motion.petVars` keys first; `CompareCustomVariable` added with the source's numeric coercion and `operatorType ?? operator ?? "Equal"` semantics.
- **`src/fx-engine.mjs`**: `getVar` exposes `pet.velocity.x/y/length`, `pet.facingLeft`, `distanceToPet` from the published `motion.pet`, and `PunchDelay`/petVars from `host.petVars`.
- **`src/render.mjs`**: line primitive handled in `sampleItem` and `drawCanvas` (stroked before atlas lookup).
- **Host wiring (`js/app.js`, `demo/app.js`)**: a `withPetHost(motion)` helper feeds the engine `armAngle` (previous render's `pose.frontAngle` — the pose resolves after items advance, so it lags one frame), `petBareHead` (no head item equipped), `petLift` (`lift.y`), and a flat-floor `solidAt` for the preview (the source queries real world tiles for trail platform edges).
- **Tests**: new `test/pet-system.test.mjs` (9 tests) covering kind lookup, trail lag, machine pet cells, flyers, PunchDelay, PetFlyWalk publication, hidden pets, the owl gap and piece boundedness. The two old pet tests were rewritten to the engine's own-clock semantics (60Hz frames, moving host, 21-tick trail flush). The leash-line finiteness checks in `demo/test/render-regression.test.mjs` and `demo/test/archive-renderers.test.mjs` now validate line geometry.
- **Bug fixed during testing**: the leash line piece was pushed without `petLine: true`, so it accumulated one piece per frame; now flagged and cleaned.

## Host contract

`advancePet(item, timeMs, motion)` reads `worldX/worldY` (feet line; the engine hosts at rig top-left, center = `[x+16, y+32]`), `dtMs` (clamped to 50ms like the source step), `vx/vy`, `facing`, `ground`, `punch.ageMs/.target`, `events` (punch ids), `petBareHead`, `petLift`, `armAngle`, `petWorld.solidAt`, and publishes `motion.pet`, `motion.petState`, `motion.petVars` (flat `PetFlyWalk.*` keys) for the FX layer and scripted renderers.

## Draw-band duplicate fix (idle capture overlaying the animation)

**Symptom**: most pets played their walk animation while a frozen copy of their captured idle sprite sat exactly on top of the animated one.

**Cause**: planner `gameIcon`s capture a pet sprite once per draw band, so 314 of 392 pet items listed the identical sprite twice (behind `u:0` and in front `u:12`; 589 duplicate records total). `normalizeCatalogItem` turned both into pet pieces, but `advancePet`'s cell override (`applyPetCell`) animates only the main piece — the duplicate kept its captured idle cell and drew after (over) the animated one. This was the risk flagged in the previous revision's Remaining-work item on `u:0`/`u:12` pairs; the catalog ships identical cells per band (verified: every duplicate pair matches on all authored fields except `u`), so the dedupe is safe.

**Fix**: `normalizeCatalogItem` now keeps one record per distinct pet sprite — equality is every authored field except the draw band (`u`). Multi-part pets are untouched (their pieces differ in cell or position; e.g. Dragon of Legend keeps its two wing positions plus back sprite, the silkworms keep all segments). Non-pet records are untouched — behind/in-front band pairs there are authored layering.

- Browser QA after the fix (proxy-deployed shipped UI, Calf Leash equipped, holding right): exactly one `RenderPet` draw call, and its cell cycles `(0,0)/(32,0)/(64,0)` while walking — the animation plays with no frozen overlay.
- **Inventory icons** (same session, `css/inv.css`): `.box .ic` inset widened 18% → 10.2% so item icons scale up to the inner edge of the box's white border (the border art spans pixels 7-24 of the 256px `box-white.png`, so 26/256 ≈ 10.16% is the largest clean square inset; 10.2% stays just inside), and the rule's `border-radius` was removed so square icons are not corner-clipped. Tool boxes keep their explicit 18% inset. Verified in-browser: computed inset 10.2%, radius 0px, icon fills 79.6% of the box (was 64%), icons do not touch the white border.

## Render verification follow-up (browser QA)

A later session ran local browser QA of the shipped UI against the local server with a headless browser (search + two-click grid equip). The originally requested side-by-side visual comparison against the live SetPlannerGT site was not performed and remains open (see Remaining work). Full measurements and screenshots: `evidence/pet-render-browser-qa.json` and `evidence/screenshots/pet-*.png`.

- **All representative pets render**: Puppy Leash (kind 8 trail), Butterfly Leash (kind 1 flyer), Calf Leash (kind 0 trail), Summer Kite (kind 4 kite + string), Alpha Wolf / Pet Blood Dragon / Crimson Thief Mask (kind 200 machines, the mask via its generated 64×64 piece) and Captain Bark (kind 38 flyWalk script pet).
- **Follow lag reproduced**: holding right, the player starts moving at t=847 ms and the pet at t=1166 ms — an observed wall-clock follow delay of 319 ms (~19-20 frames at the observed frame rate), consistent with the source's 20-tick trail flush.
- **Wolf punch lunge and hover extra**: a held punch drives the lunge toward the punch target (100+ units of horizontal travel, fist height), and at hover-at-target the machine pushes its second piece — cell `[10, row+1]`, rotated on the pet clock, additively blended — confirmed as two `RenderPet` pieces for 40+ frames with the punch held (generation confirmed; the additive piece's pixels were not isolated in a with/without comparison, so its visual appearance is recorded as not identified). The measured piece translation `my` −32→−16 is the source's state-6 `+16` hover offset (`'y': ... + (st===6 ? 16 : 0)` in the bundle), not an upward leap. The dragon machine follows the motion state directly with no extras, as ported; the current bundle ([app.8f20650106.js](https://setplannergt.com/app.8f20650106.js)) matches the port's extra emission and `+16` offset, though its machine-name gate is obfuscated, so the wolf/dragon attribution rests on branch structure plus the port.
- **Bug found and fixed — leash string dropped by the host atlas filter**: `renderAvatar` in `js/app.js` and `demo/app.js` filtered draw calls with `atlases.has(c.atlas)`; the string is a line primitive with no atlas, so it never reached `drawCanvas`. Both hosts now pass line pieces through (`c.line || atlases.has(c.atlas)`) and exclude them from the missing-atlas scan. The Summer Kite string now renders from the pet to the hand anchor. (The earlier "player invisible in world" symptom on the proxy-deployed copy did not reproduce on a local server; its cause was not isolated — see the note below.)
- **Regression check after the fix**: `npm test` 111/111; `demo/test` 65/72 with the same 7 pre-existing failures as `4fb7669`.
- The earlier "player invisible in world" symptom on the proxy-deployed copy did not reproduce on a local server; its cause on that deployment was not isolated, so it stays unexplained rather than attributed. The flat-floor `solidAt` and the one-frame arm-angle lag remain documented placeholders below — the idle screenshots do not exercise arm motion or terrain edges.

## Remaining work

1. **Live-site visual comparison**: the pet rendering was QA'd on a local server driving the shipped UI; a side-by-side comparison against the live SetPlannerGT planner — especially the arm anchor during active punching and pet motion at speed — is still open.
2. **Legendary Owl (11142) data gap**: its `parts` record lacks `pet:1` and it has no `gameIcon` pet records, so no pet pieces exist and no engine runs — the live client gates the same way (`drawsPet` requires slot-script RenderPet rules). If official data or a catalog update ships pet records, the engine will pick them up unchanged. Do not invent records.
3. **Pre-existing `demo/test` failures (7)**: unrelated to pets (lift/gait/fixture drift). Separate cleanup; do not bundle into a pet commit.
4. **u:0/u:12 gameIcon pairs**: resolved — the adapter now dedupes identical per-band pet records (see the draw-band duplicate fix above). If a future catalog ships genuinely differing cells per band, re-verify against the source's record selection before changing the adapter.
5. **`solidAt` world query**: the preview supplies a flat floor; the source queries real tiles for platform edges (trail pets walking off ledges). A world-aware `solidAt` can be wired when the demo grows real terrain.
